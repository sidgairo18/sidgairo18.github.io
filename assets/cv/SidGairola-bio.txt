Siddhartha Gairola is an ELLIS PhD student at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics (Saarbrücken) and the Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA), jointly advised by Prof. Bernt Schiele and Prof. Francesco Locatello. His PhD will be awarded by Saarland University. His research centres on representation learning for vision and vision-language foundation models, their use in visual understanding tasks such as detection, segmentation and localisation, and the interpretability of deep networks.

Previously he was a Pre-doctoral Research Fellow at Microsoft Research (MSR) India in the Technology for Emerging Markets group, where he worked on applications of computer vision, image processing and machine learning to low-cost diagnostic solutions for healthcare, advised by Dr. Mohit Jain and Dr. Nipun Kwatra. Before that he was a Research Intern at Adobe Inc. in the Media and Data Science Research group, working on image understanding, and a Research Assistant at the Center for Visual Information Technology (CVIT), IIIT Hyderabad. He completed his Master's by Research and B.Tech with Honours in Computer Science at IIIT Hyderabad, advised by Prof. P. J. Narayanan.

Siddhartha has also contributed to open-source software, participating in Google Summer of Code with Scilab in 2017 and 2018.
